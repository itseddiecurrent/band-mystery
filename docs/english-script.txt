AFTER-PARTY BAND MYSTERY
English performance draft

A dialogue and stage-direction revision of the implemented Chinese story.
Scene IDs in square brackets are references. Unlabelled paragraphs are action,
not spoken narration. Short internal lines belong to Locke. Optional scenes
and both sunset variants are included. Notebook and artwork text follow the play.
This document supplies the English game text. Formatting and scene IDs are not displayed.

========================================================================
UPSTAIRS ROOM — AFTER THE PARTY [opening]
========================================================================

The room is dim. Locke wakes on the sofa.

LOCKE
My head's spinning. What happened?

LOCKE
Right. The show last night. Tony rented this place, had everyone over to celebrate.

Back to the end of the show. The band is still backstage, packing up.

========================================================================
BACKSTAGE — LAST NIGHT [opening-backstage]
========================================================================

The show is over. Everyone packs up their gear.

WILLY
I told you to practice that last bit. You played like shit. Be grateful they didn't throw cabbages at you.

ARTHUR
Do you have to be such a jerk? I've been up past midnight all week working on that bit.

WILLY
Then it's not the practice. You're just not good enough.

ARTHUR
You sing and hit a few keys. What do you know? You sounded like a busted tape. Miko carried you tonight. Without her, that crowd would've walked.

MIKO
We sounded good tonight. Can you two leave it alone?

LILI
They always do this. The more they fight, the closer they get. You're always huddled up with Adam, too. What are you plotting?

ADAM
What plotting? We just get together to practice. We leave the personal stuff offstage. Miko and Willy broke up ages ago, and they still play together.

WILLY
Shut up, Adam. You know us well enough. Don't bring that up.

TONY
No fighting. Adam might swing a guitar at you.

ADAM
I wasn't actually going to hit anyone. That was for show. If something pisses you off, say it. Keep it in and you'll make yourself sick.

LOCKE
All right, all right. You were all great. Leave it there.

THE RENTED HOUSE — LATER THAT NIGHT [opening-memory]
========================================================================

At the party, Miko comes over to Locke.

MIKO
Locke, I'm so glad you came. You had us sounding so good out there.

LOCKE
That was you guys. The crowd didn't stop screaming all night.

MIKO
Come on, have a proper drink with me.

LOCKE
Ha, I think I've had a few too many already.

MIKO
You've been at it since rehearsal. Did you even get dinner? Sit down for a bit.

MIKO
I'll get you some water.

The memory trails off.

TONY'S ROOM [tony-arrival]
========================================================================

Locke enters the small room and turns on the light. Tony is still in bed.

========================================================================
WAKING TONY [wake-tony]
========================================================================

LOCKE
Tony. Wake up. Arthur's dead.

TONY
What? He was fine last night.

========================================================================
THE MILK [milk]
========================================================================

LOCKE
What happened after I fell asleep? Who poured this milk?

TONY
We went downstairs and kept drinking. I wasn't feeling great, so Miko brought me some milk. A few sips and I couldn't keep my eyes open. Couldn't even get up. I heard people arguing, I think. Might've been a dream.

Tony remembers the room before he fell asleep: a spare pillow beside the bed.

Now only the pillow under his head remains.

LOCKE
Where is everyone?

TONY
No idea. Call the police.

Tony tries. The call won't connect. Locke tries his phone. NO SERVICE.

LOCKE
Nothing on either phone. No Wi-Fi, either. I took a call by the stairs last night.

Tony throws back the covers, puts his feet on the floor, then stops.

TONY
I sent messages downstairs, too. Did you see Arthur fall?

LOCKE
No one was with me when I woke up. He was already down there when I found him.

Locke shines his flashlight along the rim of the glass. A few grains of white powder haven't dissolved.

LOCKE
There's powder here. Did you see Miko pour it?

TONY
No, she did it in the kitchen. I went out for some water after she put it down.

LOCKE
Could be sleeping pills. Leave the glass. Let's find everyone.

========================================================================

They leave the room with their phone lights on. The door at the end of the hall stands ajar. Cold air comes through.

TONY
Why's that open? I shut it last night.

LOCKE
Wait. Someone's down by the steps.

Locke opens the door and angles his light down. A figure lies on its side beside the backyard paving stones. Tony follows and lights the face.

TONY
Lili?

========================================================================
LILI WAKES [lili-testimony]
========================================================================

Locke goes down, crouches beside Lili and calls her name. She breathes, but doesn't respond.

After a while, she frowns and opens her eyes. Tony moves his light aside.

LILI
Locke? Why am I still out here?

LOCKE
Are you hurt?

LILI
My head's spinning. What's going on?

LOCKE
What do you remember from last night?

LILI
Adam and I were out here smoking. We heard Miko, Arthur and Willy arguing. Miko was crying. Couldn't make out the words, but they were really going at it. Then a thud inside. Glass breaking, right after.

LOCKE
Where did Adam go?

LILI
Inside. Told me to wait. He wasn't coming back, and I felt a bit dizzy, but I went up anyway. I reached for the door and it swung out into my shoulder. I stepped back and missed the step. Couldn't see who opened it. The door was in the way.

Tony crouches beside her and stays. Lili waits at the edge of the field to light Locke's way.

TONY
Locke, find that jammer. Switch it off and call the police. Hurry. I'll stay with her.

LOCKE
I'm on it. Hang on, Miko.

The police confirm it: Arthur suffocated. The head injury was serious, but not fatal.

LOCKE
Then he was still alive down there.

The police remove a tie clip from his left hand. The silver thing Locke saw earlier. Three diagonal grooves across its face.

========================================================================

TONY

========================================================================
THE MATCH [compare-clip]
========================================================================

In the photo, Willy wears the same tie clip. Arthur stands beside him, a hand on his shoulder.

LOCKE
Arthur could've grabbed it as he fell.

========================================================================
NEWS OF WILLY [willy-crash]
========================================================================

Before they leave for hospital, news comes from the police: Willy's car, found against a wall on a nearby road.

One rear wheel came off while he was driving. The car swung out of control and hit the wall. A severe head injury killed him.

The front is wrecked; the cabin is intact. The car can be recovered for examination. His phone was inside. Why the wheel came off is still under investigation.

On the phone: photos and videos, different dates. Willy beating Miko, tormenting her, threatening her. The police tell Locke and Tony what they found.

LOCKE
He kept doing it. It wasn't just once.

TONY
And we kept playing shows with him. Rehearsing with him. All that time, she was on her own.

The records go back through the abuse. Locke remembers Miko's sleeves covering her wrists. He decides to visit her and hear it from her.

========================================================================
HOSPITAL — OUTSIDE MIKO'S ROOM [hospital-arrival]
========================================================================

Dr. Kate brings Locke and Tony to the door.

DR. KATE
She's out of danger. Still very weak, though. Don't stay too long.

They go in. Locke puts what he's brought beside the bed.

MIKO
You came.

LOCKE
Thought we'd check on you. If you're tired, we can go.

MIKO
Can you sit closer?

Locke pulls his chair up to the bed.

LOCKE
How are you feeling?

MIKO
Better. Just worn out.

LOCKE
Don't push yourself. Want some water?

MIKO
No. Just sit with me.

They sit quietly.

========================================================================
MIKO'S ACCOUNT [hospital-followup]
========================================================================

MIKO
Locke. I'm sorry.

LOCKE
For what?

MIKO
The water last night. I put sleeping pills in it. Tony's milk, too.

Locke looks at her.

MIKO
The ones I take when I can't sleep. I knew you'd come looking for me when you woke up.

LOCKE
You could've told me.

MIKO
And you'd have let me go?

He doesn't answer.

MIKO
It was between me and those two. I wanted you and Tony asleep. You wouldn't have to hear it or get dragged into it. Adam knew I wanted to talk to them. He said he'd deal with Lili.

MIKO
Then it all went wrong.

LOCKE
What were you going to say to them?

Miko lowers her head and grips the blanket.

MIKO
I was pregnant. Kate just told me the baby's gone. I'd already decided not to keep it. But there were things I needed to say, with both of them there.

Locke is taken aback. He lets her continue.

MIKO
Do you think I'm awful?

LOCKE
Miko. I'm worried about you.

She looks up at him for a moment.

MIKO
I know. You've always been good to me.

He straightens the blanket where it has slipped to the edge of the bed.

MIKO
I told Willy to wait outside. I wanted to talk to Arthur first, but he kept saying I should sober up. Then Willy came in and they started arguing.

MIKO
Willy smashed his glass. Arthur tried to leave. Willy grabbed him, and Arthur stepped on the broken glass. He went straight down the stairs.

She stops. Her breathing quickens.

MIKO
I ran down and called to him. He didn't answer. I wanted an ambulance, but I didn't have my phone. Willy and I went to find it.

MIKO
When we came back, Adam stopped us. Said Arthur was dead. Kept telling Willy to leave. Go now, or it'll be too late.

MIKO
Arthur wasn't moving after he fell. Willy said not to call the police. Then he grabbed a hammer and ran out to the cars. I heard glass breaking.

LOCKE
So Willy remembered Tony's jammer? Took it, switched it on, hid it in the fireplace? Keep us from calling the police while he got away?

MIKO
I couldn't think straight. I don't know how it got to this.

AFTER THE VISIT [police-conclusion]
========================================================================

The police give Locke their working theory. Willy caused Arthur's fall and may have killed him afterward. Then he fled in the car. A rear wheel came off; he lost control and hit the wall.

LOCKE
The fall didn't kill him. Let's go through what Miko said. See if it fits.

========================================================================
THE FALL — MIKO'S VERSION [fall-reconstruction]
========================================================================

As Miko described it: Willy smashes the glass and grabs Arthur as he tries to leave. Arthur slips on the shards. His left hand tears off Willy's tie clip as he falls down the stairs.

LOCKE
The glass fits. So does the clip. Then Adam finds him dead and tells Willy to run.

LOCKE
Wait. Tony's missing pillow. Could that be part of this?

Locke and Tony go back through the statements and ask the police for the remaining test results.

REVIEW — THE DRINKS AND AUTOPSY [review-drinks]
========================================================================

LILI
Adam handed me the drink. I put it down on the windowsill, and he was still asking if I wanted another sip.

The same drug in Lili's drink and Tony's milk.

The autopsy: Arthur was knocked unconscious by the fall, not killed. He suffocated afterward. How?

========================================================================
REVIEW — THE CAR [review-vehicle]
========================================================================

The car has been examined again. Someone tampered with it before Willy drove away. The rear wheel came off on the road, and he lost control.

TONY
Willy was talking to me after the show. A few people went out the side door, carrying stuff. Right by where the cars were parked.

LOCKE
So any of us could've got to his car before the argument.

========================================================================
REVIEW — THE EXTORTION [review-extortion]
========================================================================

The police uncover a long-running hacking and extortion scheme. Adam, Willy and Arthur targeted companies. Investigators currently trace the exposure of their real IP addresses to a careless mistake by Adam.

After the leak, Willy and Arthur left the three-person chat. In the records they put together privately, only Adam's work remained. Several jobs all three had done were missing.

LOCKE
Only Adam's part. They wanted him to take the whole fall. He saw that.

========================================================================
REVIEW — THE BLACK FLECKS [review-feather]
========================================================================

Tests identify the black fragments under Arthur's left fingernails as bird feathers.

Locke opens the photo from after the show again.

LOCKE
He grabbed the tie clip when he fell. So when did he pick up the feathers?

THE REHEARSAL ROOM [rehearsal-arrival]
========================================================================

The night seems to be behind them. Locke and Tony ask Adam to come over: a few of Arthur's belongings need identifying.

Adam arrives in a gray-green jacket. Photos and reports cover the table.

========================================================================
THE CONFRONTATION [confrontation]
========================================================================

ADAM
So, what am I looking at? Arthur's stuff?

LOCKE
They were going to pin the extortion on you. Arthur and Willy. Miko told you they'd be meeting that night. You knew Willy might panic and drive off.

TONY
I saw you go out the side door after the show. His car was right there. The police say someone tampered with it before he left.

ADAM
Did you see me touch it?

LOCKE
You told Miko you'd get Lili out of the way. You gave her a drugged drink. She was dizzy before she tried to open the door.

Adam pushes the photos across the table.

ADAM
You done? You've got nothing.

LOCKE
Arthur survived the fall. The report says mechanical asphyxia.

ADAM
Then Willy killed him afterward. He still had Willy's tie clip in his hand.

LOCKE
He tore that off when he fell. But there were black feathers under the nails of that same hand. Look at the photo. You're the only one wearing them.

LOCKE
You said you never went downstairs. How'd he get hold of your jacket?

Adam looks at the photo. Says nothing.

LOCKE
You kept telling Willy to drive away. He thought he'd killed Arthur. Had no idea what you'd done to his car. We'd find the tie clip, and we'd go after him.

========================================================================
WHAT HAPPENED AFTER THE FALL [final-reconstruction]
========================================================================

Miko and Willy leave to find a phone. Adam goes downstairs. Arthur is still alive, his left hand closed around the tie clip.

Adam takes the spare pillow from Tony's room and presses it over Arthur's nose and mouth.

Arthur struggles. His left hand lets go of the clip and catches Adam's black-feathered stage outfit. The fabric tears.

Adam removes the feathers from Arthur's hand. Puts the tie clip back into the left hand. He misses the fragments beneath the nails.

Before reporting to the others in the backyard, he destroys the pillow and the torn stage clothes. Changes into everyday clothes.

========================================================================
BACK IN THE REHEARSAL ROOM [arrest]
========================================================================

Adam gets up. Tony bends to gather the photos. Adam snatches a chair and lunges at him.

Locke pulls Tony clear. The police waiting outside rush in and restrain Adam.

TONY
Us too? You'd kill us to shut us up?

Adam doesn't answer. The police take him away.

On the table, the group photo. Adam still in his black feathers.

========================================================================
THE HILLSIDE — SUNSET [sunset]
========================================================================

Miko is well enough to go out. She and Locke sit alone on the hillside, watching the sun go down.

LOCKE
At that rehearsal before you and Willy split up, your sleeve slipped when you reached for the mic. You said the bruises were from banging your arm.

And in last summer's photo, we're all in short sleeves or tank tops. You've got your cuffs buttoned down to your wrists. You dressed like that all the time back then. I didn't know he was still hurting you. Not till they checked his phone.

Miko keeps looking at the ridge.

LOCKE
You could barely get through rehearsal. That's when Arthur started coming to see you so much, wasn't it? You wanted him to talk, with Willy there. What you two said in private, I can only guess.

Wind through the grass. Locke pauses.

LOCKE
You kept looking out the window after the show. You could see Willy's car from there. Adam went out around then. Did you see him doing anything by the car?

LOCKE
Then he told Willy to drive away. If you'd seen something earlier, you might've known what he meant.

LOCKE
Willy always went for a drive after a fight. To cool off. We all knew that. But if you'd asked him to stay, Miko, he'd have listened. You didn't, did you?

LOCKE
You never told the police about the parking area, either.

Miko says nothing. The sun drops behind the ridge. She rests her head on Locke's shoulder. He puts his arms around her; she holds him too.

Fade out.

========================================================================
HOSPITAL — AFTER SUNSET [kat-miko-private]
========================================================================

They return to hospital. Locke leaves. Dr. Kate comes into Miko's room.

DR. KATE
How are you doing?

MIKO
Nearly back to normal. Thanks, Kate. I never thought you'd end up being my doctor. After all that, I was so glad to see you.

DR. KATE
I didn't expect to see you here, either. Your friends seem good to you. Did Locke just take you out on a date?

MIKO
It wasn't a date. Dating's the last thing I want after all this.

DR. KATE
Then will you tell me what really happened?

MIKO
What?

DR. KATE
The pregnancy. The baby had been dead for a while already. We found abortion medication in your blood. Hallucinogens, too. I've kept the report quiet for you, but what happened?

Miko says nothing.

MIKO
I don't even know whose baby it was. Ever since I found out, I was scared all the time. I felt dirty. Like I'd done something I could never put right. That's why I didn't want to keep it.

DR. KATE
You mean?

MIKO
After Willy and I split up, we'd still have those parties with Arthur pretty often. Just the three of us. Either of them could've been the father.

DR. KATE
The police and your friends filled me in. All that hacking, then their IPs leak and they turn on each other. Now they've got the man who killed them. All around the same time.

MIKO
What are you getting at?

DR. KATE
Both of them died that night, Miko. The night you picked to confront them, with everyone there. You really think that's a coincidence?

MIKO
So you think I planned it?

DR. KATE
Come on, you were brilliant at coding, Miko. Top of your class. You spent all that time at their place. You must've known what they were up to.

MIKO
Yeah, I knew. So?

DR. KATE
Did you have anything to do with that IP leak? And Adam. He's into you too, isn't he?

MIKO
What, you think I put him up to it? How would I even pull that off?

DR. KATE
I don't have any proof. And those bastards had it coming. But you, Miko. I just can't believe you'd do that. I don't want to.

MIKO
You really think that little of me?

DR. KATE
Forget it. I'm sorry. I'm talking nonsense. Locke seems nice, but this whole crowd will drag you in deeper. Once you're better, get yourself out, okay?

Miko doesn't answer.

End.

Short blue hair. Black-framed glasses.

ARTHUR — 27 — Acoustic guitar
Short purple hair. A gold chain onstage.
